The Studio

We shape spaces with purpose, context and character.

Led by Ar. Manali Patel, The Architales Design bridges architectural precision, interior sensitivity and construction craft. Every volume we conceive is tailored to honor the land it inhabits and the human lives that unfold within its walls.

From private residences in Ahmedabad to bespoke commercial realms across Gujarat, our practice emphasizes timeless natural materials—stone, concrete, warm timber, and sculpted light.
